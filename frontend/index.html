    About Nous

    Train a small model on your numbers. Ask what comes next.

    Forecast your series with confidence, get live answers, and talk to the model in plain English. Text chat is free.

    Text chat is free. Nous Plus ($9/month) adds GQ voice.

    What Nous can do

    • Forecast any number series with confidence ranges and recent accuracy.
    • Train on your own CSV in one click in a private workspace.
    • Read charts and images — attach a screenshot or photo and Nous describes it, pulls the numbers into a series, and forecasts what comes next.
    • Live answers for weather, market prices, space, and the moon.
    • Ask in plain English via the chat agent — it calls tools when it needs facts.
    • Backtested accuracy versus simple baselines (MAE, RMSE, and more).
    • Talk hands-free in voice with Talk mode.
    • Developer API — see the API docs.

    What's free, and what's in Nous Plus

    • Text chat is free. Reading the chat, live weather, markets, space, and the sample models do not cost anything.
    • Training needs a free account and stays under published caps.
    • Nous Plus is $9 a month and includes GQ voice: 40,000 characters of listening every month, about 45 minutes. There are no packs or add-ons. A free account starts with a small GQ allowance. Subscribe on this website and cancel any time.

                            World model simulator
                            
              
            
                            
            
                            
              A small learned world model of one series: a PCA latent state, learned
              dynamics, and K-Means regimes (weather also gets its yearly cycle). It rolls
              128 futures forward and compares your scenarios to the baseline, using the
              same random draws so the gap comes from your events, not luck. Every run
              shows an honest backtest against “tomorrow looks like today.”

                              Self-supervised world model (JEPA)
                              
              
            
                              
            
                              
              Nous can learn its own picture of a series without labels. A small JEPA
              encoder studies a window of the past and learns to predict the
              embedding of the window a few steps later, not the raw numbers.
              Those learned features join the PCA state of the world model. Nous then
              backtests both encoders on your series and only uses JEPA when it wins by
              at least 2%. Runs on the CPU in a few seconds, with no extra AI calls.
