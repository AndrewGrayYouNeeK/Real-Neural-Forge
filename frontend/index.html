            What do p and BH p mean?
            
          
              p is the chance that a pattern this strong would appear in draws even
              if the moon had no effect at all. BH p is the same number after
              adjusting for testing many patterns at once. A small value would hint
              at a real effect; "Survives" says whether the pattern holds up after
              that adjustment.
