What Neural Forge can do

  • Forecast any number series with confidence ranges and recent accuracy.
  • Train on your own CSV in one click in a private workspace.
  • Live answers for weather, market prices, space, and the moon.
  • Ask in plain English via the chat agent — it calls tools when it needs facts.
  • Backtested accuracy versus simple baselines (MAE, RMSE, and more).
  • Talk hands-free in voice with Talk mode.
  • Developer API — see the API docs.

What's free, and what uses credits

  • Text chat is free. Reading the chat, live weather, markets, space, and the sample models do not cost anything.
  • Training needs a free account and stays under published caps.
  • Voice credits are sold on this website. A free account starts with a small GQ allowance. Listening uses voice credits.
  • Payments are not live. Credit-pack checkout is in test mode and will not charge a real card.
